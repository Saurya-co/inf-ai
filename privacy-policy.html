Privacy Policy — INF ai

Effective date: [22 Sept 2026]
Contact: [0yzsiywsa@mozmail.com]

INF ai ("the App") is a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) AI study toolkit. This policy explains what data the App handles. The short version: we collect nothing — there is no account, no backend, no analytics, and no ads. Everything stays on your device except the messages you choose to send to your chosen AI provider.

1. Data we do NOT collect

No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses. No analytics, crash reporting, tracking SDKs, or advertising identifiers. We (the developer) never see, receive, or store any of your data on any server, because there is no server.

2. Data stored on your device

API keys: encrypted on your device (Fernet encryption under a random key kept in platform secure storage: Android Keystore / iOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager). Keys are masked in the UI and never included in exports.
Chats & attached files: stored in a local on-device database so your history works offline. They never leave the device except as described in Section 3.
App-lock PIN: only a salted PBKDF2 hash is stored — the PIN itself is never stored. Biometric unlock is gated by the operating system; no biometric data is accessed or stored by the App.
Settings (theme, selected model, preferences): stored locally only.

3. Data you choose to send (third parties)

When you tap Send, your message and any attached files/photos are transmitted over HTTPS directly to the AI provider you selected (e.g. Google, Groq, OpenRouter, Together, HuggingFace, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, or your custom endpoint). For long documents, only the most relevant excerpts are sent. The provider authenticates you with the API key you pasted into the App. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy — please review your provider's policy (e.g. data retention/training terms vary, including zero-retention options). We have no control over provider-side handling.

4. Device permissions

Camera (optional): used only when you tap "Take photo" to attach a picture to your chat. Photos are processed on-device and sent only to your selected AI provider.
Biometric / device lock (optional): used only for the app-unlock feature you enable in Settings.
Internet: required to reach AI providers and fetch model lists. History, settings, and saved chats work offline.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it: individual chats can be deleted in History; "Forgot PIN" resets the lock and saved keys; uninstalling the App removes all local data (automatic backups are disabled for key material). Provider-side retention is governed by the provider's policy (Section 3).

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. No personal data from anyone — including children — is collected by the developer.

7. Security

Keys are encrypted at rest; transport to providers is HTTPS-only. No security method is perfect: a rooted/compromised device or someone with physical access to your unlocked device can access local data. Protect your device lock and never share your API keys.

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated App release with a revised effective date above.
